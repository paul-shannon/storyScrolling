Fort Leavenworth, Kansas

I lived here for a year when I was seven years old. I was an awkward boy from a complicated family. I had a sometimes paralyzing speech impediment. We led the itinerant military life, with few social connections and no extended family. There were no grandparents to see, no cousins, aunts, or uncles.

As best I reconstruct, our quarters were on the second floor of the three-story building in the lower left of the photo. You can see a few trees, many automobiles, a brutalist darker building towards the top, everything is shaped by human hands to fulfill utilitarian (military) purposes.

No one explicitly schooled me in the world-view this place embodied.

But the Cold War arms race, hierarchy, the privileges of rank, isolation, some baseline anxiety were in the air I breathed.
