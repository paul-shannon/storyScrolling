Sword Fern Die-off, Seward Park

Broad swaths of sword fern, Polystichum munitum, died in 2014. In the the ensuing decade, many acres in pure stands have died, with only 10% of the plants surviving.

Where the sword fern grows in a mixed understory, mortality is about 50%.
