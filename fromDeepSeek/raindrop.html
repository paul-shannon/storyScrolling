The Journey Begins

Start scrolling to follow the raindrop's adventure

1 Formation in the Clouds

High in the atmosphere, water vapor condenses around tiny particles, forming droplets that come together to create clouds. Our raindrop begins its journey here, as one of millions of droplets in a cumulus cloud.

2 The Descent Begins

As the droplets grow heavier, they begin to fall. Our raindrop joins others, merging as it descends through the cloud. Gravity pulls it downward at increasing speed toward the Earth below.

3 Falling Through the Sky

The raindrop accelerates to terminal velocity—about 9 meters per second for a droplet of this size. As it falls, it encounters other droplets, sometimes merging, sometimes breaking apart in the turbulent air.

4 Impact on the Land

The raindrop reaches the ground, landing on a leaf in a dense forest. Some droplets are absorbed by plants, but our raindrop rolls off and joins others forming a small stream that trickles downhill.

5 Journey Downstream

Following the path of least resistance, the raindrop flows into a stream, then a river. It travels for days, passing through landscapes altered by its movement—eroding banks, depositing sediment, and nourishing ecosystems.

6 Reaching the Ocean

After its long journey, the raindrop finally reaches the vast ocean. It merges with saltwater, becoming part of a global system that will eventually evaporate and begin the cycle again.
